About Us

Welcome to Suratirtha Khejuri (সুরতীর্থ খেজুরী)

Established in 1977 at Kamarda Gholabar, Khejuri, Purba Medinipur, Suratirtha Khejuri has been a distinguished center of performing and visual arts for nearly five decades, guiding generations of learners under experienced gurus.

Affiliated from the Sarbabharatiya Sangeet O Sanskriti Parishad, our structured courses prepare students for annual board examinations, diplomas, cultural recitals, and artistic excellence.

Courses Offered

Our Departments

আমাদের বিভাগসমূহ

গান

Vocal Music

Classical Hindustani vocal training, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, folk melodies, and sur-sadhana with harmonium and tanpura accompaniment.

নাচ

Dance

Classical dance foundations, expressive mudras, rhythmic footwork, Rabindra Nritya, and creative stage choreography.

আবৃত্তি

Recitation

Bengali poetry recitation, clear pronunciation, voice modulation, breath control, and expressive stage presentation.

তবলা

Tabla

Structured percussion mastery covering Taal, Laya, Kayda, Tukda, solo performance, and rhythmic accompaniment.

অঙ্কন

Drawing & Fine Arts

Visual arts training in pencil sketching, oil pastel, watercolor, still life, landscapes, and creative fine arts.

Founder • Suratirtha Khejuri (Since 1977)

Mr. Sunil Kumar Das

শ্রী সুনীল কুমার দাস — প্রতিষ্ঠাতা, সুরতীর্থ খেজুরী

"In 1977, Mr. Sunil Kumar Das laid the foundation of Suratirtha Khejuri with a noble vision: to bring the light of Indian classical music, dance, recitation, tabla, and fine arts to every household in Khejuri and Purba Medinipur. His lifelong dedication continues to inspire generations of students."

Courses Offered

Our Departments

গান

Vocal Music

Hindustani classical vocal, Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, folk melodies, and structured sur-sadhana.

নাচ

Classical & Folk Dance

Classical dance foundations, expressive mudras, Rabindra Nritya, and creative stage choreography.

আবৃত্তি

Recitation

Bengali poetry recitation, clear diction, voice modulation, breath control, and stage presentation.

তবলা

Tabla

Structured percussion training covering Taal, Laya, Kayda, Tukda, solo recital, and accompaniment.

অঙ্কন

Drawing & Painting

Pencil sketching, pastel, watercolor, still life, landscapes, and fine arts diploma preparation.
